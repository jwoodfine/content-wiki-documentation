Research working papers and analytical frameworks produced by the MCorp research programme. Papers in this category are pre-peer-review working drafts unless otherwise noted. Each is accompanied by the mandatory preprint notice required by this wiki's public-posting policy — see [[preprint-notice-convention|The Preprint Notice]] for what that notice means and why papers are published this way.

This is a small, curated category by design: a flagship paper is a complete argument, argued end to end, and is added deliberately rather than accumulated incrementally. The category's first flagship paper for this wiki, an audit of an AI knowledge-flow pipeline using a research-then-audit agent swarm, is in preparation and will be published here once the wiki's paper-rendering support is in place.
